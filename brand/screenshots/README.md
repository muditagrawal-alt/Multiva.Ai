`05-studio-full.png` shows the viewer playing a clip of a recognisable
political figure. Putting that in public marketing implies an association the
project does not have, so it is kept here for reference only. To get a full
studio shot that is publishable, render a dub from a clip of someone who has
not been in the news and re-capture.

instances inside the frame `QtKjGIsKp` on breakpoint `WQLkyLRf1`. That section
also still carries the template's original copy ("SYNTIQ AI SYSTEMS FOR...",
